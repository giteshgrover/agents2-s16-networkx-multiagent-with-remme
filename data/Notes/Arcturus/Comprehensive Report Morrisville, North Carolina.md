# Comprehensive Report: Morrisville, North Carolina

## Executive Summary

Morrisville, North Carolina, situated in the heart of the Research Triangle Region, presents a dynamic profile characterized by a growing population, a robust economy, and a climate that offers distinct seasonal experiences. The town boasts a high median household income and a significant proportion of its residents holding bachelor's degrees or higher, underscoring a well-educated and affluent populace. Economically, Morrisville is a hub for technological and life sciences industries, benefiting from its proximity to Research Triangle Park and RDU International Airport. The climate is marked by hot, humid summers and cool, short winters, with spring and fall offering the most temperate conditions for outdoor activities. Recent climate trends indicate a warming pattern, particularly noticeable in winter months. This report provides a detailed overview of Morrisville's demographics, economy, and climate, drawing upon recent data to offer insights for residents, businesses, and stakeholders.

## 1. Demographics

Morrisville, North Carolina, is a rapidly growing municipality with a population that has seen substantial expansion in recent decades. As of 2023, the town's population stood at approximately 30,500 residents, reflecting a 2.4% increase from the previous year. This growth trajectory has transformed Morrisville from a small community of under 1,500 in 1990 to a significant urban center within the Research Triangle area. 

The demographic profile of Morrisville is notably diverse, with a significant Asian (Non-Hispanic) population comprising approximately 41.8% of residents, followed by White (Non-Hispanic) at 35.1%, and Black or African American (Non-Hispanic) at 13.1%. This ethnic composition contributes to the rich cultural tapestry of the town. The median age in Morrisville is 34.5 years, indicating a relatively young population, which is often associated with a strong workforce and family-oriented community.

Economically, Morrisville demonstrates a high level of affluence. The median household income was reported at $125,404 in 2023, a significant increase of 9.93% year-over-year. This figure is considerably higher than both the state and national averages, suggesting a strong economic base and high earning potential for its residents. The poverty rate is low, at 4.82% in 2023, indicating economic stability for the majority of the population.

Educational attainment is a key characteristic of Morrisville's residents. Approximately 97.8% of the population has a high school education or higher, with a substantial 72.7% holding a bachelor's degree or higher. This high level of educational attainment aligns with the town's position as a hub for knowledge-based industries and its proximity to major research universities.

Furthermore, Morrisville has a significant foreign-born population, accounting for approximately 35.1% of its residents in 2023. This diversity in origin further enriches the community. The majority of residents are U.S. citizens, with 79.2% holding citizenship, though this percentage has seen a slight decrease from 79.9% in 2022.

## 2. Economy and Employment

Morrisville's economy is robust and heavily influenced by its strategic location within the Research Triangle Region, a renowned center for technology, research, and life sciences. The town benefits immensely from its direct proximity to Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU) and Research Triangle Park (RTP), facilitating strong connectivity for businesses and access to a highly skilled workforce.

The employed population in Morrisville was 17,579 in 2023, showing a steady increase of 3.88% from the previous year. The average commute time to work is approximately 20.3 minutes, suggesting a well-integrated local job market or efficient transportation networks within the region.

Key industries thriving in Morrisville include advanced manufacturing, technology, and life sciences, aligning with the broader economic strengths of the Research Triangle. This focus on innovation and high-growth sectors contributes to the town's high median household income and demand for skilled labor.

Morrisville has experienced significant investment and expansion, attracting both startups and established global corporations. This economic dynamism is further supported by various incentive programs and initiatives aimed at fostering business growth and innovation within Wake County and the surrounding Research Triangle area.

The town's economic profile is further characterized by a high median property value, standing at $481,800 in 2023, with a 12.2% year-over-year increase. The homeownership rate in Morrisville is 48.8%, indicating a market where both renters and homeowners are present.

## 3. Climate and Weather Patterns

Morrisville, North Carolina, experiences a temperate climate characterized by distinct seasons, with hot and humid summers, short and cold winters, and generally wet and partly cloudy conditions throughout the year. The climate data suggests that residents and visitors should be prepared for a range of weather conditions across the seasons.

**Summers** in Morrisville are notably hot and muggy. The average high temperatures in July, the hottest month, hover around 89°F (32.1°C), with July lows averaging 70°F (21.6°C). The heat can be intense, with a mean heat index appraised at a blistering 105.8°F (41°C) in July. The hot season typically spans from late May to mid-September, with average daily high temperatures consistently above 81°F (27.2°C).

**Winters** are short and cold. January is the coldest month, with average lows around 33°F (0.3°C) and average highs of 51°F (11.6°C). While not severe, winter does bring freezing temperatures and requires preparation for colder weather.

**Spring and Fall** offer the most pleasant weather for outdoor activities. The best times for warm-weather enjoyment are from early May to mid-June and from late August to mid-October. During these periods, temperatures are more moderate, with average highs in the 70s and 80s°F (20s°C) and comfortable lows.

**Precipitation** is generally consistent year-round, contributing to the town's wet climate. While annual precipitation is stable, there can be year-to-year variability. The climate data indicates that the town is generally partly cloudy throughout the year.

**Climate Trends:** Recent climate trends show a discernible warming pattern across Morrisville. Winter seasons have experienced the most significant increase in temperature. This warming trend is consistent with broader regional and global climate change patterns, indicating a long-term shift in temperature averages.

**Impact on Daily Life:** The climate data suggests that daily life and local events need to be planned with consideration for the hot, humid summers and cool winters. Outdoor activities are best suited for the transitional seasons of spring and fall to avoid the extremes of summer heat and winter cold.
