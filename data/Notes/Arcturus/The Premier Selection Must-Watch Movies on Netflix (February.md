# The Premier Selection: Must-Watch Movies on Netflix (February 2026)

This report presents a curated list of essential films currently available on Netflix, identified for their critical acclaim, compelling narratives, and significant cultural impact. Leveraging data from Rotten Tomatoes and other reputable sources, this selection aims to guide viewers toward high-quality cinematic experiences.

## Executive Summary

Netflix's vast library offers a spectrum of cinematic achievements, from groundbreaking animations to intense thrillers and profound dramas. This report highlights ten "must-watch" films, each distinguished by exceptionally high critical ratings (primarily 99-100% Tomatometer scores) and praised for their unique contributions to their respective genres. The selection spans a variety of themes and styles, ensuring a diverse and engaging viewing experience for subscribers. These films represent the pinnacle of current Netflix offerings, reflecting a commitment to quality content that resonates with audiences and critics alike.

## Introduction to "Must-Watch" Criteria

The designation of a film as "must-watch" is based on a rigorous evaluation of its critical reception, particularly its Tomatometer score from Rotten Tomatoes, and its perceived artistic or entertainment value. For this report, films achieving a 99% or 100% Tomatometer score are prioritized. The descriptions provided are synthesized from critical consensus and synopses, offering a concise overview of each film's strengths and appeal. The inclusion of recent releases alongside critically lauded older titles ensures a dynamic and relevant selection. This approach guarantees that viewers are directed towards films that have demonstrably impressed critics and are likely to provide a satisfying viewing experience.

## Curated Film Selections

The following films have been identified as essential viewing on Netflix:

### 1. Wallace & Gromit: Vengeance Most Fowl (2024)

**Rating:** 100%

**Description:** This latest installment in the beloved Wallace & Gromit franchise offers a "charming revival" of the iconic duo. Directed by Aardman's celebrated team, including Nick Park, the film is noted for retaining the signature wit and impeccable visual gags that have defined the series. Critics have lauded it as "comforting as cheese and crackers," with added commentary on modern technology. The movie promises an "epic adventure" that appeals to both long-time fans and new audiences, reinforcing the enduring appeal of stop-motion animation and character-driven comedy. The synergy between Wallace's inventive (and often disastrous) contraptions and Gromit's steadfast loyalty continues to be the heart of these narratives, making it a guaranteed delight.

### 2. How to Train Your Dragon (2010)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** This animated masterpiece is celebrated for its "dazzling animation" and a script that delivers "surprising dramatic depth." The film tells the story of Hiccup, a young Viking misfit who befriends an injured dragon, challenging the deeply ingrained prejudices of his clan. Its narrative expertly balances thrilling 3-D sequences with a profound exploration of friendship, empathy, and overcoming societal divides. The "stunning visuals" combined with a "surprisingly deep narrative about friendship and overcoming prejudice" make it a standout in the animated genre. The film's success lies in its ability to engage audiences of all ages with its universal themes and breathtaking world-building, creating an emotional resonance that extends far beyond its fantastical premise.

### 3. Godzilla Minus One (2023)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** "Godzilla Minus One" has been hailed as a "compelling kaiju film that balances epic destruction with engaging human stories." Set in post-war Japan, the film presents a formidable Godzilla as a new crisis emerging from the nation's devastation. Critics have praised its ability to maintain audience engagement through "engaging human stories" even between the spectacular scenes of mass destruction. This approach elevates it beyond a typical monster movie, offering a narrative with significant emotional weight and thematic depth. The film's success lies in its masterful blend of spectacle and character development, making the monstrous threat feel all the more impactful due to the human stakes involved.

### 4. Are You There God? It's Me, Margaret. (2023)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** This adaptation of Judy Blume's beloved novel is described as "effervescent and refreshingly frank about the travails of puberty." The film successfully captures the essence of the "classic and groundbreaking novel" that has impacted generations. It navigates the complexities of adolescence with sensitivity and humor, offering a "frank adaptation" that resonates deeply. The movie's strength lies in its authentic portrayal of a young girl's journey through self-discovery, friendships, and family dynamics, making it a poignant and relatable experience for viewers of all ages. Its ability to address sensitive topics with honesty and warmth underscores its status as a significant cinematic achievement.

### 5. Will & Harper (2024)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** "Will & Harper" is presented as an "endearing and heartfelt" documentary that serves as an "ode and testament to long-lasting love, acceptance and evolution within a friendship." The film chronicles a deeply personal journey of friendship and self-discovery, likely focusing on the evolving relationship between its subjects. It is praised for its "heartfelt tribute to enduring love, acceptance, and the evolution of friendships," suggesting a narrative rich in emotional depth and personal reflection. The documentary's strength lies in its intimate portrayal of human connection and the transformative power of acceptance, offering a moving and insightful look at the complexities of relationships.

### 6. The Forty-Year-Old Version (2020)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** "The Forty-Year-Old Version" is a "sharp and insightful debut from Radha Blank," an artist striving for a breakthrough. The film "opens a compelling window into the ebbs and flows of the artist's life" and marks Blank as a "major filmmaking talent." It is lauded for its insightful exploration of the creative process, the challenges faced by artists, and the pursuit of authenticity in a demanding industry. The movie's sharp wit and honest portrayal of its protagonist's journey make it a thought-provoking and engaging cinematic experience. Blank's ability to blend humor with poignant social commentary is a key element of its critical success.

### 7. Under the Shadow (2016)

**Rating:** 99%

**Description:** This "skillfully crafted chiller" is recognized for its ability to "blend genres with timely themes and thought-provoking social commentary." Set against the backdrop of the Iran-Iraq War, the film masterfully combines horror elements with a compelling narrative that explores themes of fear, resilience, and the supernatural. Critics have noted its "timely themes and thought-provoking social subtext," highlighting its capacity to engage audiences on multiple levels. The movie's effectiveness lies in its atmospheric tension, strong performances, and its ability to weave a terrifying tale with meaningful social and political undertones.

### 8. 28 Years Later

**Description:** Hailed as the "latest installment in the '28 Days Later' franchise," this film promises an "ambitious and unpredictable follow-up to the zombie apocalypse narrative." Directed by Danny Boyle and featuring screenwriter Alex Garland, it is described as an "ambitious and unpredictable" continuation of the groundbreaking zombie horror. The film follows survivors navigating the perilous post-apocalyptic landscape, pushing the boundaries of the genre with its narrative. The inclusion of director Danny Boyle and screenwriter Alex Garland signals a return to the quality and innovation that defined the original film, suggesting a high-stakes and compelling cinematic experience.

### 9. The Rip

**Description:** "The Rip" is characterized as a "lean crime thriller" that "stars Ben Affleck and Matt Damon as Miami cops navigating a tense situation involving a large sum of confiscated cash." The plot centers on a high-stakes scenario where a significant amount of money is discovered, leading to escalating tension and mistrust among the team. The film is noted for its "lean" approach, suggesting a tightly plotted and suspenseful narrative. The chemistry and presence of Affleck and Damon as leads contribute to its appeal, promising a gritty and engaging crime drama.

### 10. 1917 (2019)

**Year:** 2019

**Description:** This Oscar-winning war film is renowned for its "immersive, single-shot presentation and bracing realism." "1917" follows two British soldiers tasked with delivering a critical message across enemy territory during World War I. The film's technical achievement of appearing as if shot in one continuous take creates an unparalleled sense of immediacy and immersion for the viewer. Critics have praised its "bracing realism" and the "immersive, single-shot presentation," which amplifies the tension and emotional impact of the soldiers' perilous mission. This cinematic technique, combined with a compelling narrative, makes it a powerful and unforgettable viewing experience.
